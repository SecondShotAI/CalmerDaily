Quiet nature, filmed on our family's land, plus slow views of Earth from orbit and guided meditations. A new pause every day.

A quiet place to rest and focus

Calmer Daily is ambient and calm nature content for rest and focus. Most of it is filmed on our own family land, then paired with music and, on some videos, a short meditative message.

Watch on YouTube: youtube.com/@CalmerDaily

Schedule

  • Shorts: new every day
  • Long-form: a new video every week
  • What you'll see: nature on our land, Earth from orbit, guided meditations

About the Calmer Daily pipeline

Calmer Daily uses a small, private software tool that prepares our own videos and uploads them to our own YouTube channel, @CalmerDaily, using the YouTube Data API. It is used only by the channel owner. It does not offer sign-in to the public and does not collect data from viewers.
