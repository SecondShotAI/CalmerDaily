Bologna 1284

The real history behind The Mortadella Mob: the guilds, the salt and the law of medieval Bologna.

Watch on BuildingIt Meet the series

The History Behind the Series

Bologna 1284 is the history show behind The Mortadella Mob. It tells the real story the series is built on: the pork aristocracy, the salt roads from Cervia, the market inspectors, and the punishments that kept the trade in line.

The series is "The Guilds of Salt and Steel: Cured Meat, Power, and Law in Medieval Bologna," told in eight parts, from the city of guilds to the papal decree that defined what Mortadella means.

Behind the Scenes

The behind-the-scenes show follows how The Mortadella Mob is made (the Meshy, OpenArt, ElevenLabs and Kling workflow, from stills and script to finished film) so other makers can see the whole process, tools and all.
