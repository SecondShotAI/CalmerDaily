Second Shot AI is a one-person media and AI business in Lake County, Florida. We use AI-assisted workflows to research, make and publish shows across five YouTube channels, and we build tools that AI agents can use.

See our channels See our projects
Our mission

A second shot at building something that lasts

Second Shot is about resilience, legacy and second chances. The goal is practical: build sustainable, well-run digital properties that serve their audiences with care and consistency, and grow them into a business that lasts for the next generation.

We combine human direction with automated production pipelines. Editorial choices, quality checks and final publishing decisions are made by people. The automation handles the repetitive work so the content can stay consistent.

About the Calmer Daily upload tool
