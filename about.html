About Us

SecondShotAI is a one-person media and AI business owned and operated by John Esposito, based in Lake County, Florida. It uses AI-assisted workflows to research, write, and publish content across several channels.

Our goal is practical: build sustainable, well-run digital properties that serve their audiences with care and consistency.

What We Publish

  • Calmer Daily: ambient and calm nature content for rest and focus.
  • BuildingIt: our active series channel (@StillBuildingIt), home of The Mortadella Mob and Bologna 1284.
  • SecondShotAI: scripted AI debates and AI topics.
  • SecondShotsov: our newest channel.
  • Son of a Vet (@ASonOfAVet): military history and the stories behind the service.
  • VerifyLite: pay-per-call safety checks and web utilities for AI agents.
