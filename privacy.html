Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

This policy covers the Calmer Daily pipeline (the "App"), a private tool operated by Unfallowed Ground LLC ("we", "us") that uploads videos to our own YouTube channel, @CalmerDaily. It also covers this website.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Who uses the App

Only the owner of the Calmer Daily channel. The App is not offered to the public, and members of the public cannot sign in to it.

What data the App accesses

  • With the channel owner's permission (Google sign-in), the App uploads videos, sets their titles, descriptions, tags and privacy status, and manages playlists on the Calmer Daily channel.
  • It reads basic channel information (the channel name) to confirm it is connected to the right channel.
  • It does not read, collect or store data about viewers, subscribers or any other YouTube user.

How data is stored

The App stores one sign-in token, on the channel owner's own computer, so it can upload without asking again. It also keeps a local list of uploaded video IDs and playlist IDs. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's YouTube API, and nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Device information and cookies

The App does not store, access or collect information on any viewer's device, and does not use cookies. This website does not use cookies or analytics.

Keeping and deleting data

The channel owner can delete the stored token at any time by deleting the file youtube-token.json from the App's folder, and can revoke the App's access at any time at Google security settings. Data obtained through the YouTube API is kept only as long as needed to manage the channel's uploads, and is deleted within 30 days of access being revoked.
