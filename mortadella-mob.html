Illegal Sausage in Bologna, 1284

The Mortadella Mob is a dark, gritty animated series set in Bologna in 1284, about the illegal sale of Mortadella. Tommaso da Parma, a former Guild butcher now grinding herbs, is drawn back into the trade — with his wife Chiara, their children Pietro and Gemma, and his partner Renzo — while the Butchers' Guild and its enforcer, Captain Jacopo, hunt the "Pink Ghost."

Status

The pilot is finished, and the series is in production. Watch it on YouTube at BuildingIt (@StillBuildingIt).

How It Is Made

The series is made with modern AI production tools — Meshy, OpenArt, ElevenLabs, Kling, Gemini, Muse, and VEED — with human direction on the story, the writing, and every final cut. Inspired by The Fat Electrician.
