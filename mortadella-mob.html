Animated series

The Mortadella Mob

Illegal sausage in Bologna, 1284. A dark, gritty animated series about the illegal sale of Mortadella.

Watch on BuildingIt The history behind it

The story

Tommaso da Parma, a former Guild butcher now grinding herbs, is drawn back into the trade, with his wife Chiara, their children Pietro and Gemma, and his partner Renzo, while the Butchers' Guild and its enforcer, Captain Jacopo, hunt the "Pink Ghost."

How it is made

The series is made with modern AI production tools (Meshy, OpenArt, ElevenLabs, Kling, Gemini, Muse and VEED) with human direction on the story, the writing and every final cut. Inspired by The Fat Electrician.

See how it is made on the Bologna 1284 page.
